# B5: Bonus (Monitoring & GitOps)

To secure the **5 Bonus Points** for Track B, we went above and beyond by implementing both **Metrics & Alerting** and **GitOps**.

## 1. Metrics & Alerting (Prometheus + Grafana)
We created a complete observability stack in `monitoring/docker-compose.yml`. 
- **Prometheus** scrapes metrics from our applications and infrastructure.
- **Loki & Promtail** aggregate our structured security logs.
- **Grafana** acts as the visual dashboard on top of both, allowing us to build real-time alerting rules if our application crashes or if our metrics exceed normal thresholds.

## 2. GitOps (ArgoCD)
We also provided the configuration for **ArgoCD** in `argocd/application.yaml`. 
By applying this manifest to our Kubernetes cluster, ArgoCD continuously monitors our Git repository. If a developer pushes a change to the `k8s/` folder, ArgoCD instantly and automatically syncs the Kubernetes cluster to match the repository state. This entirely removes manual deployment steps and fulfills the GitOps bonus requirement!
